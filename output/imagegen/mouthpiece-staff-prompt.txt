Anime illustration, 16:9, same style and group-portrait framing as the Incipio and Index Lab staff images. The residents of "Mouthpiece", a slum inside the sewers of a steampunk city, home to people driven out of every other city.

BACKGROUND: gray concrete sewer wall crowded with tangled pipes, valves, gauges and leaking joints. Dim lamps, a faint mist of steam.
