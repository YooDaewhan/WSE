Built-in image_gen; logo concept v4 (CRT heartbeat monitor, mirrored wordmark)

Use case: logo-brand. Reference image: public/images/starterpack/starterpacklogo.png (sibling logo; Starter Pack = an old game switched on, Rescue Pack = the same old screen in the dark, still receiving one lonely signal).

Create a retro game title logo for a Korean fictional character collective named RESCUE PACK, Korean 레스큐팩, designed as the display of an old green monochrome CRT heart monitor / oscilloscope. Wide horizontal canvas, the whole image is the dark screen itself, generous margins.
Mood: lonely, quiet, late at night. A signal sent alone from somewhere far away (the future), over and over, hoping someone answers. Rescue as CPR: a flatline that starts beating again. Hopeful, not horror.

Composition, centered and vertically symmetrical around one horizontal center line:
1. Background: near-black CRT glass #050A06 with a soft curved vignette at the corners, fine horizontal scanlines, faint phosphor noise, and a very dim green measurement grid like an oscilloscope.
2. Center line: a single glowing phosphor-green signal line #39FF6A runs across the full width. On the left it is a flat line; in the middle it jumps into one sharp heartbeat spike (an ECG QRS shape, tall up and deep down); then continues to the right as smaller pulses, the newest point at the right end is a bright dot with a short fading afterglow trail.
3. Above the line: "RESCUE PACK" in a chunky blocky pixel/terminal font in glowing phosphor green, followed by a solid block cursor "_" as if it was just typed.
4. Below the line: the same "RESCUE PACK" mirrored UPSIDE DOWN (flipped vertically across the center line), like the negative half of the waveform. Slightly dimmer, a touch of horizontal tearing / signal dropout in one or two letters, but still clearly legible as the flipped wordmark.
5. The heartbeat spike passes between and slightly into both wordmarks, so the top text rides the up-swing and the bottom text hangs from the down-swing.
6. Bottom right corner, small: Korean "레스큐팩" in a small pixel hangul, same green.

Glow: subtle phosphor bloom on the green elements only, slight RGB-free green chromatic softness, as if photographed off a real old monitor but still clean and readable as a logo. Strictly monochrome: black and shades of phosphor green (#39FF6A bright, #1E8F3C mid, #0B3A18 dim). No other colors.

Exact text only: "RESCUE PACK" (top), its upside-down mirror (bottom), and "레스큐팩". No numbers, no BPM readouts, no other words, no hearts, crosses, shields, stars, sunsets, characters, monitor bezel, hospital props, mockup, or watermark. One logo only.
