A bright, playful, and slightly clumsy Korean pop song. 
It expresses the feeling of setting out on a first journey — full of excitement, courage, and a hint of worry. 
The singer is a lively girl who pretends to be confident but secretly feels anxious, yet keeps moving forward with pure optimism. 
Upbeat tempo, shining melody, adventure and hope-filled atmosphere. 
Language: Korean.

A bright and emotional Korean pop-rock song inspired by Younha’s Silver Line.
Clear and powerful female vocal with a sense of wind and freedom.
Starts directly with vocals and gentle piano, then builds with rhythmic drums and soaring guitars.
Verses light and flowing, pre-chorus rising tension, chorus emotional and open like the sky.
130 BPM, clean electric guitars, bright piano, and spacious reverb — fresh, uplifting, and heartfelt.
Language: Korean.
